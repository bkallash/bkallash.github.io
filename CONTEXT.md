# Portfolio

A bilingual (English/Arabic) personal site for Bayan Kallash, a freelance AI engineer. It tells recruiters and clients what Bayan does within seconds, in plain language.

## Language

**Visitor**:
Anyone reading the site. There are two kinds: **Recruiter** and **Client**.
_Avoid_: user, reader

**Recruiter**:
A Visitor screening Bayan for a job.

**Client**:
Someone who hires, or might hire, Bayan for a freelance project. Itqan Consulting is a past Client, and a prospective Client is a Visitor.

**Project**:
Something Bayan built: what it does, who it was for, the stack, and the outcome. Client work and personal work are both Projects. The site has no separate work-history section.
_Avoid_: experience, job

**Featured Project**:
A recent, substantial Project with its own **Case Study** page. Currently the Invoice extractor, the Regulation chat and Fluentia. A Project gets promoted to Featured when it's bigger or newer than the current ones, and older ones move down to Minor.

**Folio**:
The system Bayan built for Itqan Consulting. It is not a Project itself. It contains two Projects, the Invoice extractor and the Regulation chat, and each of their Case Studies says it is part of Folio.

**Capability**:
A broad area of AI engineering Bayan offers, such as reading documents, answering from sources, coordinating agents, or measuring quality. Each Capability is described in plain language and points to the Projects that prove it. Capabilities speak to Clients about what Bayan *can* build. Projects show what Bayan *has* built.

**Case Study**:
A short page for one Featured Project covering the problem, the approach, the stack, screenshots and results. It never includes code or a demo when the Client hasn't allowed one.

**Minor Project**:
A Project with one line and external links only, and no Case Study.
_Avoid_: other project, side project

**About**:
Who Bayan is: a short bio, education and skills. A section on the home page, shown before the Projects. Education lives here, not in a section of its own.
